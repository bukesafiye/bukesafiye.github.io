*Son güncelleme: Kasım 2026*

Oyun açılışta yeni sürüm olup olmadığını mağazaya sorar: Android'de Google Play'in kendi güncelleme servisi, iPhone'da App Store sürüm sorgusu kullanılır. Cihazının kendi yedekleme özelliği (Android yedeklemesi veya iCloud) açıksa, işletim sistemi bu ilerlemeyi senin hesabına ait yedeğe ekleyebilir ve yeni cihazda geri yükleyebilir. Bu yedek senin hesabında kalır; biz ona erişemeyiz.

# Privacy Policy — LineStank

*Last updated: November 2026*

LineStank is made by MiroGames. The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no in-app purchases; the game plays offline.

When it starts, the game asks the store whether a newer version exists: on Android it uses Google Play's own update service, on iPhone an App Store version lookup. Only the app's identifier and your device's country code are sent; no personal information or game progress. At a suitable moment the store's own rating prompt may appear; rating is entirely up to you.

Your progress (unlocked stages and stars, medals, tank series and garage upgrades, abilities, daily rewards, quests, achievements and endless-mode records) and your settings (language, difficulty, sound effects, music, vibration, hints and damage numbers) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, and are deleted when you remove the app. Sounds are generated inside the app; vibration only uses your device's haptic motor. No data is sent to us or anyone else.

If your device's own backup is turned on (Android backup or iCloud), the operating system may include this progress in a backup that belongs to your account and restore it on a new device. That backup stays in your account; we cannot access it.

The "Privacy Policy" and "Support" links in Settings open this website in your device's browser; the game itself only contacts the store for the version check above.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
