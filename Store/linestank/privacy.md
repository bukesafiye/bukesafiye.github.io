# Privacy Policy — LineStank

*Last updated: October 2026*

LineStank is made by MiroGames. The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no network connection.

Your progress (unlocked stages, gold, permanent tank upgrades and your chosen tank series) and your settings (language, sound effects, music, vibration and in-battle hints) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you remove the app. Sounds are generated inside the app; vibration only uses your device's haptic motor. No data is sent anywhere.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
