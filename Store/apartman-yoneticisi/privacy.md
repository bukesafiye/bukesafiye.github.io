# Privacy Policy — Apartman Yöneticisi

*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

Apartman Yöneticisi is made by MiroGames. The game runs fully offline: there are no accounts, ads, analytics tools or third-party services, and no data is sent to our servers. We do not collect or sell your personal data.

**Stored on your device:** Your player name, your game progress (buildings, meters, decisions, staff) and your tone, language, sound and notification settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

**Purchases and ads:** This version has no purchases and no ads. If they are added later, payment will be handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play and this page will be updated first.

## Deleting your data

Just uninstall Apartman Yöneticisi; we hold no data about you. For questions, email [bukesafiye@gmail.com](mailto:bukesafiye@gmail.com).

We do not knowingly collect information from children. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
