# Privacy Policy — Lost & Found Office

*Last updated: October 2026*

Lost & Found Office is made by MiroGames. The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
