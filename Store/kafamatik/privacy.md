# Privacy Policy — Kafamatik

*Last updated: October 2026*

Kafamatik is made by MiroGames. The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no network connection.

Your progress (stars earned, levels reached, remaining free hints) and your sound and matchstick style preferences are stored only on your device and are deleted when you remove the app. The vibration when you solve a level only uses your device's haptic motor and sends no data.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
